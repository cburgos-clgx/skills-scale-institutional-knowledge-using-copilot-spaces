# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Specialists

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Specialists validate that delivered work meets acceptance criteria and quality standards through test planning, execution, and defect management.

### Responsibilities
- Design test plans for features, integrations, and regressions
- Execute manual and automated tests and document defects
- Validate release readiness with smoke and regression checks
- Partner with teams to improve quality practices and test coverage

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce production incidents
- Ensure releases meet agreed quality and acceptance standards
- Build confidence in delivery through reliable test signals

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, defect tickets, and test execution summaries
- Standup updates on quality risks and release readiness
- Retrospective feedback on quality improvements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on reproducible defects and testability
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria quality
- Reports quality risks and release readiness to Project Managers

---

## Stakeholders / Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business priorities, provide strategic direction, and approve major scope, budget, or timeline decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define success outcomes and strategic constraints
- Review milestones, delivery progress, and business impact
- Approve key scope changes and release decisions
- Escalate organizational dependencies when needed

### Goals
- Ensure initiatives deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between delivery and strategic priorities
- Reduce decision latency for high-impact trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Steering updates, milestone reviews, and decision logs
- Approval checkpoints for scope, budget, and timelines
- Feedback on roadmap outcomes and adoption metrics

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on priorities and outcomes
- Coordinates with Project Managers on status, risk, and approvals
- Informs Developers and technical teams through decision context

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects guide technical direction, system design, and engineering standards while supporting execution across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture patterns and technical constraints
- Lead design reviews and evaluate technical trade-offs
- Mentor Developers and drive engineering consistency
- Identify and mitigate technical risks early

### Goals
- Maintain scalable, maintainable system design
- Improve delivery quality through strong technical guidance
- Reduce long-term technical debt and rework

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision records and technical design docs
- Design review feedback in planning and PR workflows
- Risk and dependency updates with delivery leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers on implementation strategy
- Supports Product Managers with feasibility and trade-off analysis
- Advises Project Managers on technical dependencies and risk

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
The Security & Compliance Officer ensures security controls and regulatory requirements are considered and verified throughout the delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs and changes for security and compliance impact
- Define security controls and compliance checkpoints
- Coordinate security testing and incident response readiness
- Track remediation of security findings and compliance gaps

### Goals
- Prevent avoidable security incidents and data exposure
- Ensure ongoing compliance with required standards
- Embed security awareness into day-to-day delivery practices

### Typical Communication
- Security risk assessments and remediation tracking
- Compliance reviews tied to releases and audits
- Incident briefings and post-incident action plans

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers on secure implementation and remediation
- Works with QA / Testing Specialists on security validation
- Escalates security and compliance risks through Project Managers

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master / Agile Coach facilitates agile delivery practices, improves team flow, and removes process blockers to help teams deliver predictably.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and remove delivery impediments
- Coach teams on agile principles and continuous improvement
- Track flow metrics and help refine team working agreements

### Goals
- Increase delivery predictability and team effectiveness
- Reduce blockers and context-switching overhead
- Strengthen collaboration and continuous improvement habits

### Typical Communication
- Ceremony facilitation notes and follow-up actions
- Impediment tracking and escalation updates
- Retrospective outcomes and process improvement experiments

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Project Managers with cadence, planning flow, and risk surfacing
- Helps Product Managers and Developers improve backlog readiness
- Reinforces cross-functional collaboration norms across teams
