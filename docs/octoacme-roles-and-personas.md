# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / Testing Specialist

### Role Summary
QA specialists design and execute test strategies to ensure delivered features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards. They collaborate with developers and product leads to validate functionality, identify defects, and drive quality improvements.

### Responsibilities
- Design test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated testing for features and regressions
- Identify, document, and triage defects
- Validate release readiness and run smoke tests
- Participate in sprint planning and refinement to understand testing needs
- Recommend quality improvements and testing tools

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce production incidents
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Enable confidence in deployments through comprehensive testing

### Typical Communication
- Participates in standups, sprint planning, and kickoff meetings
- Reports defects and testing status in project board
- Reviews and validates PR testing requirements
- Participates in retrospectives to improve testing approach

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Developers to understand technical implementation and testing challenges
- Collaborates with Product Managers on acceptance criteria clarity
- Reports quality metrics and risks to Project Manager
- Advises on test planning during project planning phase

---

## Stakeholders / Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and sponsors are business and executive-level stakeholders who provide strategic direction, approvals, and resources. They define business priorities, measure success, and make go/no-go decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Set business priorities and strategic direction
- Approve project charter and release decisions
- Provide business context and success metrics
- Allocate resources and budget
- Receive and act on status updates and risk escalations
- Participate in project reviews and retrospectives

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and ROI
- Align project outcomes with organizational strategy
- Minimize business risk and disruption
- Make informed decisions based on project health

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Project charter reviews and sign-off
- Escalation meetings for critical risks or decisions
- Release announcements and impact assessments
- Retrospective participation for strategic learnings

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Sets priorities and success criteria for Product Managers
- Approves project scope and timelines with Project Managers
- Reviews risks and escalations from Project Managers
- Receives updates on product impact from Product Managers and QA metrics
- Provides final approval for releases and major decisions

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, mentor developers, and manage technical risks. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review and mentor developers on code quality and best practices
- Identify and manage technical risks and dependencies
- Guide technology selection and tool evaluation
- Participate in technical design reviews and spike investigations
- Advocate for technical debt reduction and refactoring
- Support developers in problem-solving and technical decisions

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and secure solutions
- Reduce technical debt and improve code quality
- Mentor and develop team technical capabilities
- Enable efficient team velocity through good architecture

### Typical Communication
- Technical design docs and architecture reviews
- Code review comments and guidance
- Technical risk escalation to Project Manager
- Participation in planning and spike estimation
- Mentoring and pair programming sessions
- Technical retrospectives and postmortems

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Mentors and guides Developers on technical implementation
- Advises Project Manager on technical risks and timelines
- Collaborates with QA on test strategy for complex features
- Works with Security Officer on secure architecture patterns
- Shares technical insights with Product Managers on feasibility

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security officers ensure projects meet security and compliance requirements. They review designs, conduct security assessments, manage incidents, and advise on risk mitigation strategies.

### Responsibilities
- Review project plans for security and compliance implications
- Conduct or oversee security assessments and code reviews
- Manage security incident response and post-incident reviews
- Advise on data protection, authentication, and authorization requirements
- Track security-related risks and mitigation efforts
- Ensure compliance with applicable regulations and standards
- Provide security training and awareness guidance

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data incidents
- Ensure projects meet regulatory requirements
- Build security awareness and best practices across teams
- Reduce security risks while maintaining velocity

### Typical Communication
- Reviews project charters and risk registers
- Participates in planning meetings for security-sensitive features
- On-call for security incidents and response
- Monthly security briefings to stakeholders
- Security assessment reports and remediation tracking
- Post-incident blameless retrospectives

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Advises Developers on secure implementation practices
- Escalates security risks to Project Manager and Sponsor
- Collaborates with QA on security test planning
- Works with Technical Leads on secure architecture patterns
- Provides guidance to Product Managers on security requirements

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate Agile ceremonies, remove team blockers, and coach teams on Agile practices and continuous improvement. They enable team self-organization and improve delivery predictability.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help team identify and remove blockers and impediments
- Coach team on Agile practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain project board and sprint visibility
- Support process improvements based on retrospective feedback
- Help resolve conflicts and foster psychological safety
- Report on team velocity and delivery predictability

### Goals
- Enable team self-organization and empowerment
- Improve delivery velocity and predictability
- Foster a culture of continuous learning and improvement
- Reduce process friction and organizational impediments

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members for coaching
- Process improvement recommendations from retrospectives
- Dashboard and velocity metrics to stakeholders
- Blocker escalations to Project Manager
- Coaching on team dynamics and Agile principles

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Supports all team members (Developers, QA, Technical Leads) in following Agile practices
- Works with Project Manager to resolve cross-team dependencies and risks
- Coaches Product Managers on backlog refinement and prioritization
- Facilitates communication and collaboration across all roles
- Helps retrospectives identify actionable improvements
